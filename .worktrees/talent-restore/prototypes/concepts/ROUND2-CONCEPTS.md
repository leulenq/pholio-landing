## 1 · EVERYTHING PHOLIO DOES FOR A TALENT (codebase-verified)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Seven pillars. Every claim below is shipped in pholio-app unless marked.

### I. THE BOOK — your portfolio, curated like an agency would
- Real AI (Groq vision) reads every frame: shot type, style, expression,
  market signals; talent confirm or correct each read.
- Book-gap coaching: "a side view clarifies shape and proportion" —
  portfolio-gap and package-intelligence analysis, coverage segments.
- Frame editor: professional crops (2:3, 4:5, 1:1, 16:9), shoot metadata.
- Per-photo image-rights & model-release tracking (rights status, license,
  photographer, copyright owner).
- Submission packages (up to 50 frames) assembled from the book.

### II. THE MATERIALS — agency-grade artifacts, composed for you
- Comp card: 2-page PDF whose layout is *composed from the photograph
  itself* — palette derived from your hero shot's pixels, geometry solved
  from the image's quiet zones, focal-aware joint-safe crops.
- Digitals sheet PDF. Saved presets that never silently redesign.
- Stats formatted US/EU automatically; nudges before export if stats are
  incomplete; watermarked exports (toggle).
- (Designed, not yet live: an Apple Wallet identity pass — your card in
  your pocket. Tease only if we want a "coming" beat.)

### III. THE ADDRESS — a public home for the work
- Hosted book at pholio.studio/yourname — live today.
- Studio+ gets the chrome-less "portfolio-pro" presentation: *the book is
  the page*.
- First-party website analytics (Studio+): visits, unique visitors, page
  views, outbound clicks, day-by-day — your own traffic, owned by you.
- FUTURE (sell as "coming to Studio+"): a full personal website *built
  for you* from what Pholio already knows — your frames, your card, your
  story. No web designer, no template shopping.

### IV. THE WORDS — you sound as professional as you look (Studio+ AI suite)
- Bio writer (generate/refine), training & experience summary writer,
  submission-note writer (draft/sharpen/shorten the note agencies read
  first), message polish for agency threads.
- This is a differentiator no template site has: the platform writes
  *with* you in industry language.

### V. THE CIRCULATION — the market, reachable and honest
- Apply to agencies from inside the book; board-fit hints per agency;
  digital-slot matching maps your frames to each board's required shots.
- Open-call invitations lead the list (and bypass the monthly cap).
- Honest industry lifecycle: in review → shortlisted → kept on file (a
  soft yes — never buried with rejections) → interview → signed.
- Interview scheduling with "ball in your court" prompts; per-application
  message threads; email + in-app notifications on every status change.
- Representation records: mother agency vs placement, market/territory/
  division, exclusivity — a working model's real rep map.
- INBOUND: agencies discover you — semantic search over your actual look
  (text + image embeddings), gated entirely by YOUR controls.

### VI. THE INTELLIGENCE — see the room from the booker's side
- Pulse: "Four agencies reviewed your materials. Your card was pulled
  eleven times — most from Paris."
- Seismograph (attention over time as ink), Rhythm Field (when attention
  arrives, day × hour), Market Board (which markets watch), Book Ranked
  (your frames ranked by the attention each earns), Trajectory (90-day
  momentum, weights always inspectable), Stage Clock (review latency vs
  the platform band — silence reframed as normal), Signal Spectrum (a
  mix, never an invented score), Pipeline Flow (funnel + diagnosis:
  materials vs targeting vs follow-through).
- Connected socials (Instagram/TikTok/YouTube) pull real follower reach
  into the profile agencies see.

### VII. THE PROTECTION — control is a feature
- You choose: public-portfolio toggle, agency-discovery toggle, blocked-
  agencies list, contact visibility, per-field visibility (rolling out).
- Minors: cryptographic guardian consent gates measurements, body
  imagery, public sharing, card export; body-imagery AI coaching is
  suppressed without consent.
- Watermark exports, session list with per-session revoke, JSON data
  export, pause vs permanent delete. Rights tracked per photograph.

### Studio+ (one quiet line wherever it appears)
$9.99/mo · 14-day free trial. Free: 7-day intel window, 5 discovery
submissions/month. Studio+: full intel ranges + zones, unlimited
submissions, the AI writing suite, portfolio-pro site + analytics, and
(coming) the built-for-you website.

### Never claim
Talent-side "recommended agencies with explanations" (not shipped),
guaranteed signing/fame, any single score, the Wallet pass as live, the
website builder as live (it is "coming to Studio+").

## 2 · THE INDUSTRY LENS — what makes a talent platform feel REAL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

What a working or aspiring model actually carries:
- **Comp cards cost real money** — $75–300 through print shops or
  designers, re-done every time stats or hair change. Pholio composes
  them from the book, endlessly, for the price of a coffee subscription.
- **Submissions vanish into silence.** The single most demoralizing part
  of the job. A serious platform doesn't promise callbacks; it makes the
  silence legible (Stage Clock) and the soft-yes visible (kept on file).
- **Digitals go stale.** Agencies want fresh digitals every few months;
  models forget; opportunities quietly rot. (Agency Lens = freshness of
  every material an agency wants.)
- **The industry is full of predators.** Pay-to-play "agencies," scam
  scouting, stolen images. Trust signals are not legal boilerplate — they
  are product: rights tracking, guardian consent, blocked agencies,
  watermarks, honest language.
- **Feedback is gatekept.** Nobody tells you which frame works. Book
  Ranked and the per-frame AI reads are the first honest mirror most
  models get.
- **Vocabulary is the shibboleth.** Book, digitals, comp card, boards,
  open call, on file, go-see, mother agency, placement. One wrong word
  ("apply for jobs!", "win the pipeline!") and the page reads as built
  by outsiders. Equally: one invented "talent score" and we're a scam.

Two audiences, one page: the aspiring model needs to feel *guided*
("you don't need a book yet — one headshot is enough to begin") and the
working model needs to feel *equipped* (fresh materials, real analytics,
more markets, less admin). The story must rise from guided → equipped,
which conveniently is also the product's own arc.

Tone laws: never promise fame; promise *professionalism and visibility*.
Never gamify. The platform is the calm, competent back office — the
model is the talent. Pholio is staff, not stage.

## 3 · THE STORY WE SELL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

**Central claim: "The agency behind the agency. Everything a top
agency's back office does for its best faces — Pholio does for you."**

The end-to-end arc (this is the scroll spine for every concept):

1. ARRIVE — one headshot is enough; the Scout reads you like a booker.
2. BUILD — the book takes shape with an editor at your shoulder (AI
   reads, gaps, coaching, rights).
3. FORM — the materials compose themselves: card, digitals, presets.
4. SOUND — the words get written with you: bio, notes, messages.
5. STAND — the work gets an address: pholio.studio/you, the book is the
   page; (coming) a whole website built from it.
6. MOVE — the market opens: boards, open calls, honest statuses,
   interviews, threads; and the market comes to you, on your terms.
7. SEE — you finally watch the room watch you: pulls, markets, rhythm,
   your strongest frames — a mix you can read, never a score.
8. SAFE — running under everything: consent, rights, control. (Not an
   act — a thread visible in every act.)
9. BEGIN — the ask: begin your casting. 14 days on the house.

### CONCEPT D — DROPPED (user decision 2026-07-08). Kept for record.
### "A YEAR IN THE LIFE" (axis: TIME — the working diary)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
**Premise.** The page is a model's first year on Pholio, told as a
dated working diary — DAY 001 to MONTH 12. Scroll advances time. The
viewer doesn't read about features; they watch a career assemble.

**Protagonist device: THE TABLE.** A studio flat-lay seen from above —
one persistent scene that ACCUMULATES. Every chapter adds a real object
to the table: two digitals → the growing book → the comp card → the
digitals sheet → a phone showing pholio.studio/you → letters and
telegrams (statuses) → the intel broadsheet. The scroll's final move:
the camera pulls up and the full table reads as one composition — the
complete kit Pholio built around one person. (This is "sell everything"
made literal: by the end, everything we give you is *on the table*.)

**Acts & pacing** (P = pinned scrub, S = fast sweep):
- P0 Arrival: a mono date stamp types "DAY 001". Gold underline draws.
- P1 DAY 001 — ARRIVE: an empty table; two digitals slide in; the Scout
  reading typesets beside them. "One headshot is *enough* to begin."
- S  date-flip sweep (days tick past like a call-sheet counter).
- P2 WEEK 03 — BUILD: frames land on the table one by one as the AI
  annotates each in mono; gaps appear as empty gold-hairline outlines
  that fill as coaching lines are followed. "A book, built with an
  *editor* at your shoulder."
- P3 WEEK 05 — FORM: the card composes itself from the book's own
  pixels — swatches lift off the hero photo, the layout solves, the
  card prints onto the table. "Composed from the *photograph* itself."
- S  sweep: the words act rides the transition — bio lines type and
  refine themselves in the margin as time passes (SOUND, compact beat).
- P4 MONTH 02 — STAND: a phone/frame object lands showing your address;
  visits tick in mono beneath it. Quiet line: "Coming to Studio+: the
  whole website, *built* from your book."
- P5 MONTH 03 — MOVE: envelopes arrive and depart across the table;
  statuses stamp in honest language; one envelope stamped KEPT ON FILE
  gets the caption "a soft yes — never filed with the rejections."
  An interview card flips to "ball in your court."
- P6 MONTH 06 — SEE: the intel broadsheet unrolls across the table:
  Pulse sentence set huge, Seismograph ink-wash draws, Stage Clock arc,
  Signal Spectrum band. "Watch the room *watching*."
- S  MONTH 12 — pull-back: camera lifts, the whole year on one table.
  The protection thread is revealed literally — a gold thread that has
  been running under every object connects them; mono footnotes name
  the safeguards (rights · consent · your controls).
- P7 BEGIN: table clears to one blank card + CTA "Begin your *casting*."

**Set pieces.** (P2) hover any frame → the AI's read for that frame
typesets live with a leader line. (P5) drag an envelope to flip it —
its status and thread preview on the back.

**Motion stack.** Entirely DOM/SVG/CSS-3D: custom lerped scroll + GSAP
ScrollTrigger; the table is a perspective-transformed plane with
objects as layered elements; SplitText-style reveals; SVG line-draws.
No WebGL needed. Risk: LOW. The emotional register: warmest of the
three; strongest for aspiring talent; time axis makes "end-to-end
support" the literal plot.

### CONCEPT E — "ISSUE 001: YOU" (axis: PRINT — the editorial issue)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
**Premise.** The page IS a fashion editorial issue about one model —
masthead PHOLIO, "ISSUE 001 — [YOU]". Every offering is a *spread* in
the magazine the industry would publish about you if it could see you
properly. The intelligence lives as the magazine's marginalia: the AI
is the invisible editor annotating every spread in mono. Deepest
commitment to the editorial-modeling aesthetic; motion carries it so it
never becomes a static zine.

**Protagonist device: THE ISSUE.** Spreads turn horizontally (full-
viewport page-turn sweeps between pinned spreads); a persistent folio
line (mono: issue no · section · page) tracks position like a running
head. The gold underline is the section rule on every spread.

**Acts & pacing:**
- P0 COVER: near-black cover; masthead PHOLIO sets; cover lines typeset
  one by one — and the cover lines ARE the offering list (The Book /
  The Card / The Address / The Words / The Season / The Market Report).
  A cover portrait develops from grain. "This is the issue the industry
  never *printed* about you."
- S  page-turn.
- P1 CONTENTS: an animated contents page — each line draws its gold
  leader dots... (no; dots are taboo) — each line draws a gold hairline
  to its page number; hovering a line previews its spread in a small
  frame. (Set piece.)
- P2 SPREAD: THE BOOK — photo-essay spread; frames enter as tipped-in
  prints; the AI's mono marginalia annotate shot/style/expression in
  the gutter; a gap in the essay is shown as an empty frame with the
  coaching line beneath. "Read like a *booker* reads."
- P3 SPREAD: THE MATERIALS — still-life product spread: card + digitals
  sheet art-directed like a perfume ad; swatches lift from the photo to
  the card's palette. "Composed from the *photograph* itself."
- P4 SPREAD: THE WORDS — a typography-only spread: a rough bio line is
  struck through and re-set, live, into industry English; submission

- P5 SPREAD: THE ADDRESS — full-bleed like a fashion-house ad: just

- P6 SPREAD: THE SEASON — the submissions diary as a society column:

- P7 SPREAD: THE MARKET REPORT — data-journalism spread: Pulse headline

- P8 THE FINE PRINT — the colophon as the protection story: rights,
  consent, your controls — set small, dignified, completely readable.

- P9 BACK COVER: ink again; the subscription ask: "Begin your
  *casting*." — 14 days on the house.

**Set pieces.** Contents-line hover previews; (P3) drag to rotate the
card still-life; (P4) a "refine" scrub the user can also click through.

**Motion stack.** DOM + GSAP; horizontal page-turn transitions
(translateX + slight skew/shadow, not a cheesy 3D flip); SplitText
reveals; SVG figures. Optional deluxe: WebGL page-curl. Risk: LOW-MED.
Register: the most *industry* of the three — it flatters the trade and
positions Pholio inside fashion culture, not adjacent to it.

### CONCEPT F — "MAISON PHOLIO" (axis: SPACE — the house tour)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
**Premise.** Pholio as a physical maison — the agency house that works
for you. One continuous camera dolly moves room to room; each room is
one pillar, staffed by an invisible intelligence (light behaves like
attention). "An agency of one — with the whole *house* behind you."
The most oryzo-native: a spatial tracking shot where the page never
feels like sections at all.

**Protagonist device: THE LIGHT.** A warm gold light (the house's
intelligence — and the visitor's presence) travels with you: it scans
frames in the studio, warms the print room, glows in the window, sorts
the mail. The light IS the AI — omnipresent, calm, never a mascot.

**Acts & pacing** (rooms are pinned; corridors/stairs are fast sweeps):
- P0 THE DOOR: near-black; a gold hairline draws a tall doorframe; the
  wordmark sets as the brass plate. The door opens into—
- P1 THE STUDIO (Book): frames hang in a dark studio; the light sweeps
  each one and mono reads typeset beside them; an empty hanging frame
  glows with a coaching line. "Read like a *booker* reads."
- S  corridor sweep (hairline architecture lines rush past).
- P2 THE PRINT ROOM (Materials): the card composes on a press bed —
  palette lifts from the photo, geometry solves, the gold border draws;
  a digitals sheet stacks beside it. "Composed from the *photograph*
  itself."
- P3 THE WRITING DESK (Words): a desk lamp pool; bio lines type,
  strike, re-set; the submission note sharpens. "Sound as professional
  as you *look*."
- P4 THE FRONT WINDOW (Address): the camera turns — you look OUT
  through the maison's window: your name on the glass reversed,
  pholio.studio/you glowing on the street side, passersby as moving
  light traces (= your visit analytics ticking in mono). "The work gets
  an *address*." + coming-to-Studio+ website line.
- S  stair sweep down.
- P5 THE MAIL ROOM (Circulation): pigeonholes; envelopes route along
  gold hairline paths to city labels (Paris/Milan/Tokyo/NY); statuses
  stamp honestly; KEPT ON FILE goes into its own pigeonhole — visibly
  NOT the bin. An interview telegram slides out: "ball in your court."
- P6 THE MAP ROOM (Intelligence): the war-room table: Market Board as
  cartography, Seismograph as a seismic drum drawing, Stage Clock as a
  wall clock whose band glows, Spectrum as a brass gauge — all drawn as
  ink/brass instruments, zero dashboard. "Watch the room *watching*."
- P7 THE VAULT (Protection): a quiet, short room: rights ledgers,
  a consent seal, your keys (session/controls) on hooks. One line:
  "Your book. Your rules. *Always*." (The trust beat gets a whole room
  — in this industry that's a selling feature, not compliance.)
- P8 THE STREET: doors open outward into pre-dawn light; the maison's
  window glows behind you; CTA: "Begin your *casting*." The gold
  underline is the horizon line.

**Set pieces.** (P1) pointer = a second light that develops any frame
it touches. (P5) drag an envelope to a pigeonhole. (P4) the window
traces react to pointer movement.

**Motion stack.** Layered DOM parallax "rooms" (3–4 depth layers each)
+ GSAP ScrollTrigger camera moves (scale/translate on room containers);
light as radial-gradient layers + masks; SVG hairline architecture;
SplitText. Deluxe path: WebGL light/shadow pass. Risk: MED — the
spatial continuity demands the most art direction, but nothing here
requires WebGL to prototype. Register: most cinematic and most
"platform as staff, model as talent"; the house metaphor makes the
full-service story physical.
