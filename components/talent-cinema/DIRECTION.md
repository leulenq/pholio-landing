# /talent — "The Read" (design direction)

## Thesis
In this industry your work is judged silently. Pholio does the judging **in
front of you** — types your photos, checks your set, art-directs your card,
audits your package, and shows you who leaned in — before any agency sees a
thing. The page is that argument, told in four acts, in the machine's own
voice. It is not a product tour and it never hard-sells: every claim is
checkable in the product.

## Acts
1. **READ — "Know what they see."** (ink) A portrait is read on stage:
   reticle brackets draw, mono readouts tick in (typed shot, set check,
   digitals age), a serif verdict lands. PITS is the opening act because it
   is the product's sharpest differentiator. CTA above the fold.
2. **MADE — the card.** (cream) The comp-card engine as what it really is: a
   computational art director (image forensics, contrast validation,
   unobstructed-face gates, nine editions, eight typographic voices, seeded
   takes). Scroll flips the real engine renders.
3. **SENT — the dossier.** (ink) The 7-page application, compressed; the
   package audit runs before send. The one-studio claim resolves here:
   made, checked, and sent from the same place (media · card · applications
   · public book).
4. **SEEN — the signal.** (cream) Per-recipient tracked links first, then
   the seismograph. Footnote: under 20 views Intel says "too early to read"
   — it never invents a number.
5. **Close.** (ink) Single dock action + three checkable promises:
   applying is free · your images stay yours · leave with everything.

## Visual DNA (talent dashboard, re-cut for marketing)
- Canvas: continuous ink #050505 (#111 panels), warm-white text tiers; one
  cream act (MADE) + one cream act (SEEN) for register alternation.
- Ledger voice: JetBrains Mono uppercase micro-labels as **live readouts** —
  the page's signature motif.
- Serifs: Noto Serif Display 300 for marketing headlines (one italic-gold
  verdict word); Playfair only for lines the product itself speaks.
- Inherited motifs: grayscale→color develop ("being seen"), gold hairline
  sweeps, flat-cornered frames on ink, solid-gold marquee words (no
  gradient-clip), 3px-radius ink buttons / gold underline links.
- New motifs (born from the DNA, not copied from screens):
  - **Reticle brackets** — thin gold L-corners that draw around a frame as
    it is read.
  - **Readouts** — mono lines that tick in like a wire feed, with caret.
  - **Measure lines** — 1px hairlines with mono end-labels drawn across a
    frame (the machine measuring).
  - **The assembly** — the card composing itself on scroll (frames arrive,
    the name lockup sets, an edition flips).

## Motion grammar
Premium and purposeful: every scroll-tied move is **the machine working** —
never decoration. Draws/measures use EASE cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1);
arrivals use the app spring {stiffness:55, damping:16}; readouts tick on
staggered thresholds. Scroll-driven setpieces are sticky stages with phase
states (the house pattern). `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses everything to
quiet fades; content is never gated behind motion.

## Bans (unchanged)
No onboarding theater, no unshipped features (Apple Wallet has no backend —
never mention it), no badges/checkmarks/testimonials/urgency, no feature
grids, no pill CTAs, one italic-gold word per headline, falsifiable copy
only.
