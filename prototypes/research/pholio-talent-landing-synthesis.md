# Pholio talent landing: research synthesis and implementation plan

**Date:** 2026-07-12  
**Status:** research direction; no prototype implementation authorized in this phase

## Decision

The new landing page should be built as one continuous film about the life of a model’s work.

The central creative device is:

> **One photograph becomes a working Book, a comp card, a submission dossier, and evidence for what the talent does next.**

The product position beneath that film is:

> **Pholio is the talent-owned professional source of truth for a modeling career.**

The clearest public-facing category language is:

> **The working Book for a modeling career.**

“Operating system for talent careers” remains a useful internal strategy and a late-page conclusion, but it is too abstract for the opening. Models should first understand what Pholio does in their language: it keeps their Book, digitals, card, profile, submissions, and career signals connected.

## The four findings that determine the design

### 1. The real product advantage is continuity

The audit found twenty meaningful talent capabilities, but the advantage is not their count. It is the data spine connecting them.

A photograph can be uploaded once, classified, dated, cropped, credited, rights-cleared, placed in the Book or a digitals set, hidden from an audience, used in a card, selected for a dossier, shown publicly, and measured for attention. The same structured profile feeds public presence, comp cards, agency discovery, submission readiness, and Intel.

That makes the defensible transformation:

> **Scattered work → industry-readable Book → comp card → rights- and consent-bound dossier → agency workflow → intelligible career signal.**

### 2. Models buy readiness, legitimacy, and reduced uncertainty

Industry research shows that a professional identity is modular, not a single portfolio:

- current unretouched digitals;
- a considered Book showing range;
- a portable comp card;
- accurate stats and booking information;
- credits, tearsheets, campaigns, and motion where relevant;
- a current, controlled way to send the right package;
- follow-through after it leaves.

Aspiring talent need to feel legitimate without being sold a fantasy. Working talent need currentness, speed, control, and continuity across agencies and markets. Both care more about being properly prepared than about generic “creator tools.”

### 3. The strongest marketing story is transformation, not taxonomy

Six narrative frameworks were compared. “The Book That Moves” is the best fit because it can contain the complete product without becoming a feature tour.

Its advantage is causal progression:

- the image gains professional meaning;
- that meaning composes the right artifacts;
- the artifacts enter a real workflow;
- uncertainty is held honestly;
- recorded signals return;
- the returned evidence changes the next edit.

This is stronger than “from aspiring to professional,” which can sound patronizing to experienced talent and imply an outcome Pholio cannot guarantee.

### 4. Oryzo’s premium quality comes from directed continuity

The fresh browser study showed that Oryzo treats its section elements as ranges on one film timeline. One product persists while camera, light, material, context, crop, typography, and surrounding world transform. The next scene routinely exerts force before the current scene resolves.

Pholio needs the same governing system, not the same visual effects:

- one persistent subject;
- one scroll state;
- continuous camera and object coordinates;
- inherited composition between acts;
- overlapping transitions;
- alternating movement and stillness;
- a closing loop that changes the meaning of the opening.

## Conflict resolution

The research did not produce one perfectly aligned answer. The following choices resolve the important tensions explicitly.

### “Operating system” versus industry language

**Conflict:** the brand brief wants a modern operating system; industry research warns that abstract infrastructure language does not explain the immediate value.  
**Decision:** lead with the Book and the package. Earn “career system” late in the story by showing continuity across preparation, submission, signal, and representation.

### Agency access versus talent ownership

**Conflict:** discovery and agency workflow are emotionally powerful, but centering agency approval can reduce the model to someone waiting to be chosen.  
**Decision:** the talent’s readiness and control are the resolution. Agencies decide outcomes; Pholio ensures the work enters correctly, the package remains traceable, and the talent understands recorded movement.

### Living source versus frozen send

**Conflict:** “update once and everything stays current” is broadly true, but submitted dossiers are intentionally snapshotted and must not mutate after sending.  
**Decision:** visualize a fork. The living Book continues to evolve; at the send moment a deliberate, frozen edition separates from it and travels into the agency workflow. This limitation becomes proof of professional rigor.

### Intelligence as hero versus intelligence as payoff

**Conflict:** Talent Intel is one of the most differentiated capabilities, but leading with analytics or AI would make Pholio resemble generic SaaS.  
**Decision:** intelligence appears first as quiet judgment—classification, currentness, rights, package readiness—and becomes explicit only after the dossier enters the market. Intel is the intellectual climax, not the opening claim.

### Aspiring entry versus professional credibility

**Conflict:** a beginner-friendly story can alienate working models; a jargon-heavy story can intimidate new talent.  
**Decision:** begin with one headshot as a low-friction entry, then use real terms correctly: digitals, Book, comp card, board, open call, go-see, kept on file, mother agency, placements, bookouts. Explain through transformation rather than glossary copy.

### Luxury abstraction versus product comprehension

**Conflict:** premium fashion communication benefits from restraint and abstraction; product marketing requires specificity.  
**Decision:** each cinematic passage lands on one concrete proof frame. Poetry creates the premise; exact product behavior verifies it.

### Oryzo-level spectacle versus a fashion product

**Conflict:** Oryzo uses synthetic product rendering, technical parody, intense post-processing, and long novelty passages. Those would make Pholio feel like hardware, crypto, or an AI demo.  
**Decision:** transfer continuity, camera grammar, timing, and compositional inheritance. Replace hardware spectacle with editorial photography, paper, ink, crop systems, darkroom light, and restrained intelligence overlays.

### Comp card as signature object versus whole-career story

**Conflict:** the comp card is the strongest single visual transformation, but Pholio is more than a card generator.  
**Decision:** make the card the first major “arrival” moment, then let it gather into a dossier and reappear later as a card pull signal. It is a recurring artifact, not the endpoint.

## Positioning hierarchy

### Core category

**The working Book for a modeling career.**

### Transformation promise

Pholio turns current images and career facts into the materials agencies actually use, carries a deliberate package into participating agency workflows, and separates meaningful movement from raw attention afterward.

### Three proof pillars

1. **Your work becomes industry-readable.** Digitals remain distinct from styled Book work; frames carry date, role, credits, rights, and audience; stats and materials are checked for currentness.
2. **Your Book becomes the exact artifact the moment requires.** Public portfolio, digitals PDF, print-ready card, discovery preview, and agency-specific dossier share one source while preserving frozen editions when sent.
3. **What happens after sending becomes more legible.** Review, advance, requests, go-sees, kept-on-file states, card pulls, qualified attention, and raw reach are not presented as the same signal.

### Supporting proof

- adult talent can opt into agency Scout discovery;
- direct agency applications and open-call entry use a structured seven-part dossier;
- application messages and interview responses stay attached to the submission;
- mother agency, placements, availability, and bookouts extend the system beyond the first application;
- public, agency, and image-level visibility decisions remain under talent control.

## Copy direction

### Recommended opening

**Your work enters the room before you do.**

Supporting line:

> Pholio turns a living Book into current digitals, a comp card, and the exact dossier an agency receives—then shows you how the submission moves.

This is more product-specific than “Your book should do more than sit there,” while retaining the “Book That Moves” concept beneath the surface.

### Recommended closing

**Your work. In motion.**

CTA:

**Build your Book**

Secondary option:

**Start your Pholio**

### Voice

The voice should resemble an excellent Book editor:

- exact;
- calm;
- visually literate;
- industry-aware;
- never overeager;
- never promising representation or bookings;
- willing to leave space.

### Claims that can appear

- “Digitals stay digitals. Styled work stays in the Book.”
- “Pholio reads the frame, suggests its role, and lets you confirm the call.”
- “Your stats and current materials are checked before a package can leave.”
- “A print-ready card is composed from your real images, proportions, and stats.”
- “See the exact dossier the agency will receive.”
- “Review, advance, card pull, qualified attention, and reach are different signals.”
- “You decide what is public, what agencies can see, and which images travel.”

### Claims that must not appear

- AI can determine modeling potential, beauty, market fit, or the perfect agency.
- Pholio guarantees discovery, callbacks, representation, bookings, or income.
- named agencies can be identified from anonymous viewing behavior.
- all measurements or social metrics are verified.
- Pholio is a flexible website builder.
- Pholio manages castings, contracts, commissions, or payments end to end.
- every comp card is human handcrafted or mathematically unique.

## Continuous-film narrative

The design may use internal scene names for production, but the viewer should not perceive page sections.

### Act 1: Recognition

The experience opens on one current headshot in a quiet, near-black editorial field. The photograph is not yet presented as a software upload. It feels like raw potential with real material presence: paper edge, subtle grain, controlled light.

Copy asks the viewer to consider what enters the room before they do.

The gold underline begins beneath PHOLIO, then extends almost imperceptibly into the image’s crop baseline. It becomes the first continuity line.

**Stillness:** 2–3 viewport beats of minimal change.  
**Next scene pre-lap:** faint classification marks and a second offscreen frame begin to influence the crop before the opening copy leaves.

### Act 2: The Edit

The hero image rotates a few degrees and joins other frames in a moving contact sheet. The camera travels across, between, and briefly into images. Frames do not slide in as cards; they are discovered in a larger editorial surface.

Quiet intelligence appears as the sheet resolves:

- digital or Book;
- headshot or full length;
- current or due to refresh;
- styled or natural;
- rights ready or held private.

The talent’s confirmation is visible. The system proposes; the person makes the final editorial call.

**Concrete proof hold:** “A styled portrait cannot stand in for a digital.”  
**Next scene pre-lap:** the contact sheet’s gutters thicken into a Book spine before the classification labels fully recede.

### Act 3: The Book takes form

The contact sheet folds spatially into a Book spread. Categories inherit their place: Digitals, Book, Tests, Campaigns, Tearsheets, Motion. A small number of page turns reveal curation and range, not a carousel of features.

The camera alternates between full spread and macro detail. Credits, dates, rights, and audience behave like editorial marginalia.

**Concrete proof hold:** one organized source feeds the public portfolio, card, and submission preparation.  
**Next scene pre-lap:** the hero photograph begins to dominate the right page, and safe-crop marks appear before the Book closes.

### Act 4: The artifact

The Book closes into the proportions of a 5.5 × 8.5 comp card. The existing hero image becomes the front; other selected frames move behind it; stats align to an industry-readable back. Paper, crop, and print geometry create the first strong arrival moment.

The card briefly occupies still center frame. This is legitimacy made visible.

It then rotates edge-on. One edge expands into the shareable portfolio on one side and the card’s frozen edition record on the other. The visual establishes that outputs share a source but can preserve versions.

**Concrete proof hold:** print-ready card composed from current images and stats.  
**Next scene pre-lap:** a thin addressed sleeve begins forming around the card before the card scene ends.

### Act 5: The exact send

The card gathers with digitals, stats, selected Book frames, note, rights, and consent. These are not seven cards. They are layers of one dossier, aligning around the persistent image.

Readiness checks behave as spatial constraints: a missing current full-length digital prevents the sleeve from sealing; an expired right holds one frame outside; talent action resolves the block.

At the send moment, the visual forks:

- the living Book remains behind, breathing slowly;
- a frozen dossier edition separates and travels forward.

**Concrete proof hold:** “See the exact dossier the agency will receive.”  
**Next scene pre-lap:** the agency/board address appears while the readiness layer is still visible.

### Act 6: The room

The dossier accelerates into a dark spatial field. The camera follows closely, then stops. The dossier continues away until it becomes a thin line or distant card-sized trace.

This act must include real stillness. Pholio cannot control the agency’s decision, and the page should not manufacture instant validation.

Sparse status language appears at different depths: under review, requested more, shortlisted, go-see, kept on file. The dossier may pass through one, but the sequence must not imply every user advances.

**Tension:** the viewer waits with the object at a distance.  
**Next scene pre-lap:** a single returned pulse begins before the silence is fully released.

### Act 7: What returns

The pulse travels back along the gold line and separates into a signal hierarchy. Review, advance, card pull, qualified attention, and raw reach occupy distinct depths and weights. Reach remains farthest and least visually authoritative.

The camera moves through the hierarchy rather than revealing an analytics dashboard. Stage clock, material currentness, market/source context, and next moves appear only when defensible data is available; calibrating states remain honest.

The original photograph reappears inside the signal field. A card pull references the comp-card state; a request for new digitals references the opening image; a stale-material signal changes the crop baseline from gold to cream.

**Concrete proof hold:** “Views are not the signal.”  
**Next scene pre-lap:** signal points begin rearranging into the original contact sheet grid.

### Act 8: The loop

The signal field collapses back into the Book. Returned evidence changes the next edit: a stale digital moves to refresh, a stronger frame rises, an agency request attaches to its dossier, or a kept-on-file state remains visible without being treated as failure.

Representation, market placements, availability, and bookouts appear as subtle extensions beyond the first yes. They prove that Pholio remains useful after submission without opening a new feature chapter.

The camera returns to the original photograph, now visibly connected to a complete system. The PHOLIO wordmark and gold underline return to their opening position.

Closing copy: **Your work. In motion.**

The end reinterprets the beginning: the photograph was never a static portfolio image. It was the source of a working career record.

## Motion system

### Global state

Use one master progress value for the complete film. Scene ranges expose local progress, but no core choreography should depend on isolated `IntersectionObserver` entries.

Each frame derives:

- camera position, zoom, and focus;
- protagonist object matrix;
- background world state;
- foreground annotation state;
- typography role and mask;
- color and light state;
- transition inheritance.

Scroll reversal must reverse the narrative coherently.

### Spatial layers

| Layer | Content | Motion behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Background | photographic field, paper world, agency void, signal atmosphere | slowest; largest scale changes |
| Middle ground | photograph, contact sheet, Book, card, dossier | primary subject; camera-linked |
| Foreground | crop marks, classifications, readiness, rights, status, signal labels | quickest; sparse; mask-driven |
| Fixed chrome | PHOLIO wordmark, gold underline, minimal progress cue | nearly still; changes role rarely |

### Transition requirements

- The incoming act begins 15–25% before the outgoing act resolves.
- Every handoff preserves at least one visual property: object, crop, shape, axis, light, material, or label.
- No core transition uses a full-screen fade to hide a scene replacement.
- Major scale changes expose product meaning: frame detail, print geometry, package contents, distant workflow, returned signal.
- Type can be masked, cropped, inherited, or spatially anchored; repeated fade-up copy is prohibited.
- Each acceleration passage must be followed by a legible hold.

### Pacing map

| Master progress | Action | Feeling |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10% | one photograph; opening claim | stillness and recognition |
| 7–24% | contact sheet and classification | curiosity; slow build |
| 19–39% | Book and comp-card composition | confidence; acceleration |
| 34–55% | portfolio/card editions and dossier | precision; composure |
| 49–66% | send and agency room | transport into tension |
| 61–84% | signal hierarchy returns | discovery; measured intelligence |
| 79–100% | Book updates; career continuity; CTA | release and closure |

### Curve direction

- camera transport: long cubic/quintic in-out curves;
- crop alignment and artifact composition: critically damped springs with negligible overshoot;
- dossier launch: short acceleration with a long deceleration into distance;
- silence: scroll distance with minimal visual delta;
- returned signal: quick arrival, slow settle;
- text masks: asymmetric timing, entering slightly before object arrival and leaving after object departure.

### Reduced motion

Reduced-motion mode should preserve the causal story using discrete but connected states:

- no continuous camera flight;
- cross-state morphs limited to opacity and small scale;
- no shader distortion;
- no inertial scroll interception;
- all proof copy remains accessible in document order;
- the persistent photograph still links every state.

## Desktop and mobile are separate cuts

### Desktop cut

- wider contact-sheet travel and lateral camera moves;
- deeper parallax among paper, photograph, and annotations;
- one brief horizontal montage during the Book-to-card act;
- larger type used as scenery behind the protagonist;
- longer agency-room stillness;
- high-resolution image and paper textures.

### Mobile cut

- protagonist remains closer to the center line;
- depth is expressed through scale, focus, folding, and masks instead of wide horizontal travel;
- contact sheet becomes vertical film-strip navigation with short lateral offsets;
- annotations occupy top and bottom safe zones, never crowding the image;
- hover interactions become tap or scroll states;
- shorter travel distances but equal narrative beats;
- reduced simultaneous layers and shader cost;
- dossier layers stack in depth, not side by side;
- signal hierarchy uses vertical depth and weight rather than a wide spatial map.

Mobile is not a responsive stacking of desktop scenes. It is a retimed camera cut using the same protagonist and causal loop.

## Visual direction

### Palette

- black and near-black for the Book, agency room, and signal space;
- warm white and cream for paper, proof, and emotional release;
- restrained gold for continuity, confirmation, and key signal paths;
- muted silver/graphite only when required for print or interface legibility.

Gold should behave as a precise tracking color, not a luxury wash.

### Typography

Use a high-contrast editorial display face paired with a precise grotesk or neo-grotesk. Display type can become scenery and cropping structure. Functional copy remains compact and exact.

Avoid:

- thin all-caps everywhere;
- generic oversized SaaS headlines;
- long centered paragraphs;
- gratuitous monospaced “AI” styling;
- fashion typography that sacrifices comprehension.

### Image world

The photography must feel like real modeling materials, not generic fashion moodboards:

- honest current digitals;
- styled Book/editorial frames;
- tearsheet or campaign context;
- motion stills;
- paper and print macro details;
- contact sheets and crop artifacts.

The same featured talent should persist across the film so transformation feels causal. If multiple talents are used, they should appear after the central loop is established, not replace the protagonist every act.

## Technical direction, without implementation

### Recommended stack

- semantic DOM for all text, controls, and accessible proof content;
- GSAP timeline/ScrollTrigger or an equivalent master-timeline system for deterministic range choreography;
- a deliberately tuned smooth-scroll layer with native-scroll synchronization and immediate reduced-motion bypass;
- WebGL or Canvas only for the persistent photograph/paper object, camera, masks, depth, and a limited number of meaningful transitions;
- DOM/CSS transforms for typography and annotations;
- image sequences or carefully optimized video only where live 3D provides no semantic advantage;
- responsive scene configuration rather than one timeline with CSS breakpoints alone.

The goal is not to match Oryzo’s technical weight. It is to match its direction. A hybrid 2.5D system can achieve the required continuity with better performance and more authentic photographic texture.

### State model

Define the protagonist as one persistent state object:

- source image and crop;
- position, rotation, scale, depth;
- artifact form: loose, sheet, Book, card, dossier, trace, signal, returned frame;
- audience state;
- currentness state;
- classification state;
- rights/readiness state;
- source versus frozen edition relationship.

Scene logic changes that state; it does not mount a new visual duplicate at every act.

### Performance budget principles

- one persistent high-quality render surface, not multiple simultaneous WebGL canvases;
- preloaded hero assets with progressive lower-resolution fallbacks;
- texture eviction after completed passages where reversal constraints allow;
- no scroll-jank from layout reads inside frame loops;
- transform/opacity-only DOM motion where possible;
- device-tier shader reduction;
- mobile image dimensions and scene durations authored independently;
- performance test on a mid-tier phone before visual polish is considered complete.

## Prototype plan

Do not resume work by polishing the existing HTML. It encodes the wrong model: eighteen static sections with mostly intersection-based reveal behavior.

Build three motion studies from a shared truth and asset set. Each should be a self-contained HTML prototype, but each tests a different continuity grammar rather than a different color theme.

### Prototype A: The Book That Moves — recommended

Tests the complete one-image loop: photograph → contact sheet → Book → card → dossier → signal → returned edit.

Success criterion: the persistent frame is never perceptually replaced, and no chapter boundary feels like a new webpage block.

### Prototype B: The Room Before You

Emphasizes the package’s travel into the agency room and the honest silence afterward. The visual anchor is the frozen dossier edition and its gold tracking line.

Success criterion: agency tension is strong without suggesting guaranteed review or making approval the model’s source of worth.

### Prototype C: The Living Contact Sheet

Emphasizes editorial craft and professional currentness. The camera moves inside one evolving contact sheet that folds into artifacts and later reorganizes from returned evidence.

Success criterion: product intelligence is legible without showing a dashboard or “AI magic” effect.

After review, one grammar should be selected and expanded. Do not combine all three indiscriminately.

## Implementation phases after approval

### Phase 1: Motion greybox

- one persistent rectangle as the photograph;
- master scroll clock;
- complete camera path;
- all eight act overlaps;
- desktop and mobile configuration;
- no production imagery or polish.

Gate: the experience must already feel like one film in grayscale.

### Phase 2: Product-proof integration

- connect each transformation to one audited product truth;
- implement living source versus frozen dossier fork;
- add exact copy and claim guardrails;
- validate the sequence with aspiring and working-model perspectives.

Gate: a viewer can explain what Pholio does without seeing a feature list.

### Phase 3: Art direction

- final photography and retouching;
- paper, print, darkroom, and signal visual systems;
- typography roles and masks;
- PHOLIO wordmark and gold underline continuity;
- tonal tension/release.

Gate: still frames are premium enough to work as an editorial campaign, even before motion.

### Phase 4: Choreography polish

- tune scroll feel, curves, overlaps, holds, acceleration, and reversibility;
- add limited pointer/touch response;
- refine scene inheritance;
- eliminate decorative motion.

Gate: every moving property has a narrative reason.

### Phase 5: Production hardening

- mobile device testing;
- reduced motion;
- keyboard and screen-reader document order;
- performance budgets;
- asset failure fallbacks;
- cross-browser validation;
- product and legal claim review.

Gate: the premium experience survives outside a powerful desktop machine.

## Acceptance criteria

The page is ready only when all of the following are true:

- the same visual protagonist can be identified from opening to closing;
- every major transition begins before the prior act fully ends;
- at least three layers move with different purpose and amplitude;
- there are deliberate periods of minimal visual change;
- movement reverses coherently with scroll;
- no core proof is presented as a static feature grid;
- no product screenshot is pinned beside explanatory copy as the dominant pattern;
- intelligence is shown through defensible judgment and signals, not generic AI styling;
- the living Book and frozen submission edition are visually distinct;
- aspiring talent understand how to begin;
- working talent recognize real industry objects and states;
- no claim exceeds the audited implementation;
- mobile choreography is authored, not collapsed;
- reduced motion retains the full story;
- the closing image changes the meaning of the opening image.

## Final creative brief

> Follow one photograph as Pholio gives it professional meaning, turns it into the materials agencies actually use, sends a deliberate edition into the room, and returns recorded evidence for what the talent does next.

The page should not say, “Here are Pholio’s features.”

It should make the viewer feel one piece of work becoming a career system.
