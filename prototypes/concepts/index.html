PHOLIO

Three ways to film being seen.

An end-to-end read of oryzo.ai's motion craft, and three concept prototypes for the Pholio talent landing page that apply its grammar to our story. Each concept is a scrollable HTML prototype — open them full-screen and scroll slowly.

What oryzo.ai actually does

Oryzo (built by Lusion) is a scroll film, not a page: scroll position is a clock, and every scene is choreographed against it. Under the hood it is an Astro site with a fully custom interaction stack — notably, no scroll library at all.

LayerWhat they useWhat it does
ScrollCustom virtual scroll — wheel/touch hijack with lerp + damping One smoothed scroll value drives everything; inertia makes every scrubbed scene feel liquid. No Lenis, no ScrollTrigger.
TypeGSAP + SplitText Headlines split to lines/chars and set physically — masked reveals, staggered settles, timed to scroll beats.
3DThree.js, GLB models, custom ShaderMaterials One continuous hero object travels the whole page; noise + bloom passes; a velocity-texture fluid sim powers the "hover hand" set piece.
2D animationRive canvases per section Vector scenes (harvesting, text moments) where WebGL would be overkill — cheap, crisp, art-directed.
ArrivalPreloader canvas The world is established before the first scroll; the load itself is a scene.

The grammar worth stealing

  • One protagonist object. The product never leaves the screen; the camera moves, not the page. Continuity is what makes it feel filmed rather than assembled.
  • Pacing alternation. Long pinned chapters (multi-viewport scrubbed sequences) alternate with fast unpinned sweeps. The rhythm — hold, release, hold — is the luxury signal.
  • Playable set pieces. Roughly one pointer-driven moment per act (fluid-sim hover, encode/decode flip, temperature slider). You don't just watch the film; you touch it.
  • Right tool per scene. WebGL only where dimensionality earns it; Rive for flat scenes; DOM type everywhere else. Nothing is heavier than it needs to be.
  • Copy timed like dialogue. One line per beat, arriving exactly when the visual lands. The copy is the soundtrack.
CONCEPT A

The Darkroom

Photographic development as the metaphor for being seen. The page is one continuous exposure: it opens in a darkroom where the AI works, and mid-scroll — as your comp card assembles — the whole page develops from near-black to paper cream. Dark is where you're read; paper is where you circulate.

  1. Arrival — wordmark letters set like type in a chase; the gold underline draws and lifts into the header.
  2. The book opens — dark hero; giant serif headline over three grayscale frames drifting at different parallax rates.
  3. The scan (pinned) — a gold scanline sweeps a portrait; above the line the image is developed, below still latent; mono readings typeset in the margin as the line passes. Set piece: a pointer loupe that develops the image early under your cursor.
  4. The develop (pinned, signature) — readings collapse into a comp card assembling mid-air while the page background develops dark → cream behind it.
  5. Circulation (pinned horizontal scrub) — the card multiplies into paper artifacts: in review → shortlisted → kept on file → interview → signed, the gold underline drawing beneath the strip.
  6. Intel — editorial spread: the Pulse sentence set huge with counting numerals; Seismograph ink-wash and Stage Clock arc drawn by scroll.
  7. Close — the page dips back to ink; the card returns as a keepsake; one last underline draw under the CTA.

Scroll-film behavior: three long pinned chapters (scan, develop, circulation) separated by unpinned parallax sweeps; one full-page background-color develop as the structural midpoint; a vertical gold thread on the page edge fills as overall progress.

Production stack: custom lerped virtual scroll (or Lenis) + GSAP ScrollTrigger pins/scrubs · SplitText masked line reveals · clip-path develop · CSS 3D card assembly · SVG line-draw intel artifacts. Entirely DOM/SVG — no WebGL required. Lowest risk, ships fastest.

CONCEPT B

One Card

Oryzo's continuous-protagonist grammar applied literally: a single comp card is the hero object of the entire page and never leaves the screen. Every act is a moment in the card's life — blank paper, exposure, the read, the flip, circulation, the pull — staged in one warm dark studio where the card's cream paper is always the brightest thing in frame. The object being filmed is you.

  1. Arrival — a blank cream card breathes in dark space. "It doesn't exist yet."
  2. Exposure (pinned) — your headshot develops into the frame; the serif name stamps; the camera orbits.
  3. The read (pinned) — archetype axes orbit the tilted card as thin arcs drawing to different lengths — a reading, not a score.
  4. The flip (pinned) — one slow 180° turn to the contact-sheet back: your whole book on the back of one card.
  5. Circulation (pinned) — copies peel off along gold hairline paths toward city desks; status words return along the same paths.
  6. The pull — full-screen beat: "An agency in Paris pulled your card at 2:14 AM." Silence reframed as normal.
  7. Close — the original returns complete, takes a small bow as the CTA appears.

Scroll-film behavior: one fixed 3D card element whose rotation, position and scale are driven continuously across the entire scroll — acts scroll behind and around it; nearly the whole page is pinned chapters, with the single unpinned "pull" beat as the release. Set piece: the card is grabbable — drag to tilt with inertia, peek the front mid-flip.

Production stack: custom virtual scroll · one persistent CSS 3D card (deluxe path: WebGL paper shader + soft shadows) · GSAP master timeline mapped to scroll · SplitText · small canvas for studio dust/light. Medium risk; the strongest product-object story.

CONCEPT C

The Signal

Intelligence-first: the page is told from the attention's point of view — the market's gaze made visible as a field of gold particles that condenses into your portrait. Every act shows what Pholio lets you see that models normally never see: who looked, from where, when, for how long. Deep ink throughout, with one cream interlude — the card, the thing they're all looking at.

  1. Arrival — a single gold particle drifts in, then hundreds; they form the gold underline itself beneath the wordmark.
  2. The gaze (pinned, canvas) — the particle field condenses with scroll into the silhouette of a portrait. Set piece: the pointer disturbs the field; the gaze reacts to you.
  3. The ledger (fast sweep) — a cascade of mono event lines ("02:14 · card pulled · Paris") flying past at varied speeds; a few snap to center and hold.
  4. The card (cream interlude, pinned) — the noise cuts to silence; one comp card, composed quietly.
  5. The map of attention (pinned) — editorial cartography: city names in serif at different scales, gold pulse traces, the period scrubbing 7d → 90d as cities grow and shrink.
  6. The spectrum (pinned) — five tiers as segments of one gold line shifting mix with scroll — "never a score, a mix you can read" — then the Stage Clock arc.
  7. Close — the particles return and gather into the underline one final time beneath the CTA.

Scroll-film behavior: canvas scenes and DOM acts share the same scrubbed clock; rhythm is inverse to Concept A — dark signal world with a single cream still-point at the center; the ledger sweep is the one fast unpinned release between pinned chapters.

Production stack: WebGL particle field (~5k points, image-mask targets; 2D canvas in the prototype) · custom virtual scroll · GSAP ScrollTrigger for DOM acts · SVG line-draw traces · SplitText. Highest technical ambition; the most "modern intelligence" of the three.

Round 2 — the whole offering.

Round 1 proved scroll-film mechanics but centered the comp card. Round 2 starts from a complete codebase inventory — seven pillars of what Pholio does for a talent (the Book, the Materials, the Address, the Words, the Circulation, the Intelligence, the Protection) — and an industry lens. Full story & specs: ROUND2-CONCEPTS.md. Central claim: "The agency behind the agency — everything a top agency's back office does for its best faces, Pholio does for you." Each concept tells that story along a different axis. (A third track, "A Year in the Life," was explored on paper and dropped by decision — see ROUND2-CONCEPTS.md.)

CONCEPT E · AXIS: PRINT

Issue 001: You

The page is a fashion editorial issue about one model. The cover lines are the offerings; an animated contents page; then spreads — The Book (photo-essay with the AI's marginalia in the gutter), The Materials (perfume-ad still life), The Words (a bio struck through and re-set live), The Address (full-bleed fashion-house ad for pholio.studio/you), The Season (submissions as a society column, "kept on file" pulled as a quote: a soft yes), The Market Report (data journalism), and The Fine Print — the protection story told as a dignified colophon.

Scroll-film behavior: pinned spreads with horizontal page-turn sweeps and a persistent mono folio line; a hover-to-preview contents page; drag-to-rotate card still-life; scrubbed live rewrite in The Words.

Production stack: DOM + GSAP page-turn transitions (translateX + skew + shadow edge) · SplitText · SVG ink figures. Low–medium risk. The most inside-the-industry — positions Pholio within fashion culture, not adjacent to it.

CONCEPT F · AXIS: SPACE

Maison Pholio

One continuous camera dolly through Pholio as a physical agency house working for you: the Studio (a gold light reads your hanging frames), the Print Room (the card composes on a press bed), the Writing Desk, the Front Window (your address glowing street-side, passersby as living analytics), the Mail Room (envelopes routed to Paris · Milan · Tokyo, kept-on-file in its own pigeonhole — visibly not the bin), the Map Room (intel as ink-and-brass instruments), the Vault (protection gets its own room), and out to the Street for the ask. The traveling gold light is the house's intelligence.

Scroll-film behavior: pinned rooms built from layered parallax planes with dolly-in camera moves, handed off through door/corridor sweeps — never a hard cut. Set pieces: the pointer as a second light that develops frames; window traces reacting to the pointer; envelope flips.

Production stack: layered DOM parallax rooms + GSAP camera choreography · SVG hairline architecture · light as masked radial-gradient layers · SplitText. Medium risk, highest ceiling — the full-service story made physical.
