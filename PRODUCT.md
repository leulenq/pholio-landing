# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences, two entrances. **Talent** (models, actors, creatives — many young, many first-time, many scam-burned) arriving from Instagram/TikTok or an agency's open-call link, deciding whether Pholio is legitimate and whether it will make them look professional. **Agencies** (bookers, scouts, directors) evaluating whether the talent pool and tooling are industry-real. The marketing site's job is conviction, not documentation: visitors decide in one scroll whether this is a real industry instrument or another SaaS portfolio host.

## Product Purpose

Pholio is a verified-talent portfolio platform: cinematic onboarding ("the casting call"), AI casting analysis, a comp-card composition engine, honest attention analytics, and a professional application flow to real agencies. The marketing site exists to make the product's own atmosphere visible before signup — each page should feel like the product stepped out onto the street, not like a brochure about it.

## Brand Personality

Editorial, cinematic, withheld. The confidence of a top agency's book: it does not oversell, it shows. Three words: **editorial · cinematic · exacting**. Emotional goals: for talent — being seen, taken seriously, safe; for agencies — professional respect, signal over noise.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing grammar: feature-icon grids, hero-metric stat rows, testimonial carousels, trust badges, "New/AI-powered" chips, pricing matrices with checkmarks, rotating-word heroes, floating particles.
- Scam-adjacent modeling-site tropes: urgency ("limited spots"), vague trust adjectives ("secure", "trusted"), paid-visibility promises.
- Anything that would read as a template: the page must be unguessable from the category alone.

## Design Principles

1. **The product is the proof.** Persuade with real product compositions (onboarding stage, first-card reveal, dashboard book, comp-card editions, intel charts) rendered faithfully — never abstract illustrations of them.
2. **Motion = arrival.** Every scroll-tied move must reveal, direct, or reinforce; rest states are earned. One ease: cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1); product frames may use the app's spring (stiffness 55, damping 16).
3. **Withheld copy.** One headline (one italic-gold verdict word), one sentence per beat. Mono micro-labels carry the clerical layer. The imagery does the persuading.
4. **Falsifiable promises over trust language.** Say the checkable thing ("applying is free", "we never invent a number") instead of adjectives.
5. **Three colors only.** Velvet ink, cream, gold. Palette discipline is the brand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Honor `useReducedMotion()` / `prefers-reduced-motion` on every animation (crossfade fallbacks — the product itself does this everywhere). Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast on both ink and cream fields. Content never gated behind scroll-triggered reveals. Semantic headings; alt text on all imagery.
